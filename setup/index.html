Setup guide

Setting up Trefn.

For whoever looks after your Microsoft 365. It takes about 10 minutes, once, and then a site owner spends another 10 minutes tightening one or two lists. Nothing is installed - Trefn runs in the browser and keeps its records in SharePoint.

Before you start.

  • Someone who can approve appsfor your whole organisation - often a Global Administrator, or your IT provider. They approve once, for everyone.
  • A SharePoint sitewhere the records will live, and owner rights on it. A new team site called "Trefn" works well - only people who can open the site can see the records.
  • Your trial keyfrom our email. If you don't have it yet, skip that step and add it later from the Licence page.
  • About 10 minutesin a normal browser - Edge or Chrome - signed in with your work account.

Set up, step by step.

Set up walks you through these steps one screen at a time. Nothing is created in SharePoint until step 5, and it never deletes anything.

  1. Sign in.Go to app.trefnltd.com and sign in with your work account.
  2. Approve Trefn for your organisation.You'll see "… hasn't approved Trefn yet". Press Approve (admins). Microsoft shows what Trefn asks for and that Trefn Ltd is a verified publisher - press Accept.
    • Not an admin? Copy the link on that screen and send it to whoever is. Once they've approved it, press It's been approved - check again and carry on.
  3. Choose where your records live.Pick the SharePoint site from the list.
    • Not in the list? Open Not in the list? Paste the site's address and paste it from your browser. It looks like https://yourcompany.sharepoint.com/sites/Trefn
    • In a brand-new Microsoft 365, SharePoint search can take a few hours to start - pasting the address works straight away.
    • Want a new site? In SharePoint: Home → Create site → Team site, call it "Trefn" and add your team. Then come back and choose it.
  4. Choose what to try.Tick the packs you asked for. Only their lists are made, and you can add another later.
  5. Create the lists.Press Set up. Trefn makes its lists, with their columns, on the site. You become the first admin in Trefn. Running it again only adds what's missing.
  6. Add your trial key.Paste the key from our email and press Check and save. The screen shows your organisation's Microsoft 365 tenant id - that's what the key is made for. No key yet? Skip - add it later in Licence.
  7. Share the link.The last screen gives you a link to the site's Trefn. Send it to your team - they sign in with their normal work account. Trefn also finds the site for anyone who can open it.

One more step for a site owner.

Trefn keeps sign-offs and the investigation diary as records that are only ever added to. SharePoint has no switch for "add only", so a site owner sets it up once, in SharePoint, after Set up has made the lists. It takes about 10 minutes (5 without Trefn Quality).

With Trefn Quality: do parts A to C. Without it: just part C.

A. Make an "add only" permission level

  1. Open the site → Settings (the cog, top right) → Site permissions → Advanced permissions settings.
  2. In the ribbon: Permission Levels → Contribute → at the bottom, Copy Permission Level.
  3. Name it Trefn add only. Untick Edit Items, Delete Items and Delete Versions. Leave the rest ticked → Create.

B. Make two lists add only for members

  1. Settings → Site contents → hover over Trefn signatures → ⋯ → Settings.
  2. Permissions for this list → Stop Inheriting Permissions → OK.
  3. Tick the box beside the site's Members group (the box, not the name) → Edit User Permissions → untick everything except Trefn add only → OK. Leave Owners and Visitors as they are.
  4. Do the same for Trefn investigation.

C. Make the settings list read only for members

  1. The same way, open Trefn settings → Permissions for this list → Stop Inheriting Permissions.
  2. Tick Members → Edit User Permissions → only Read → OK.
  3. Grant Permissions → add the people who are admins in Trefn → Edit → Share. Site owners already have it.

Site owners can still change or delete those rows in SharePoint. Trefn shows it if they do, and the list's version history and recycle bin show who.

What Trefn asks for.

Trefn signs in as each person and acts as them - it can only do what that person can already do in SharePoint. It has no permissions of its own, and Trefn Ltd never receives your records.

Microsoft showsWhy
Sign in and read user profileTo sign each person in and show their name.
Create, edit, and delete items and lists in all site collectionsFor Set up to make Trefn's lists, and for people to add and change records - on sites they can already open.
Read all users' basic profilesAsked the first time someone picks a person, for an owner or a sign-off. It covers basic details only - names, email addresses and photos.

Adding a pack, removing Trefn.

Adding a pack

In Trefn, Licence → Your packs → Add to the trial emails us. When the new key arrives, paste it in Licence, then Make its lists runs Set up again on the same site. Nothing you already have changes.

Removing Trefn

Your admin can remove it at any time: entra.microsoft.com → Enterprise applications → Trefn → Properties → Delete. The lists stay on your SharePoint site with everything in them - delete them there if you no longer want them.
