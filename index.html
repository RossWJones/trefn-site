The problem

How growing businesses get stuck.

No one plans a messy system. You add a spreadsheet for a new client, a shared folder for quotes and a board to track jobs. Each fix makes sense at the time. Two years later, nothing talks to anything else, and the business runs on memory, inbox-digging and key people holding it all in their heads.

Your team doesn't have an effort problem - they're fighting their tools.

The aim is simple: make the work visible.

Questions

Questions people ask.

Is this bespoke software, or ready-made?

Both. The packs are ready-made and run in your own Microsoft 365 - you can try them free for 60 days. A bespoke system is built for your business alone - the screens your team needs to run its work.

What is in the packs?

Quality (complaints, non-conformances, changes, audits, supplier approval and training), Projects, Sales, Operations and Marketing. They share one Home, one list of what is yours to do, and the same customers, suppliers and products. See the packs.

With the packs, where does our data go?

Into a SharePoint site you choose, in your own Microsoft 365. Trefn's servers never receive it. Your Microsoft 365 admin approves the app and can remove it at any time, and you can export everything whenever you like.

We have outgrown our spreadsheets. Is Trefn for us?

Probably. Most work starts where good people are fighting bad systems: spreadsheets, shared folders, email trails and three answers to the same question. Trefn moves that into one connected system.

Will it replace the software we already use?

It can. Sometimes the right answer is to connect the software you already use. Sometimes it is to replace the tools that cost more than they give back - one connected system instead of several separate subscriptions can save a business a lot of money. The first conversation works out which.

What kind of businesses is it for?

Growing businesses where work passes between people and teams - for example manufacturing, installation, field service, logistics and wholesale. If the answer to "where is that up to?" lives in spreadsheets, inboxes and people's heads, it is worth a conversation.

Who will we work with?

Me. You don't get passed down a chain of account managers. The person learning your process is the person building the system.

Where are you based? Do you come on site?

Nottingham. I work on site or remotely with businesses across the UK - whichever suits the job best.

How does it start?

With the process everyone hates. The first conversation is free and there's no software pitch. Tell me what's slowing the team down and where information goes missing, and I'll tell you honestly whether I can fix it.

How is it priced?

The packs have a price list: £149 a month for the first, £99 a month for each extra one, and £399 a month for all five - per company, with everyone in it able to use them. Bespoke systems are all different, so there is no price list: after the first conversation you get a written proposal with a fixed price within a week. No day rates. No estimates that grow.

What happens once it is live?

It keeps improving. The system changes because the business changes, so support is agreed in the proposal - fixes, improvements and changes as the business grows.
