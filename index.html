The problem

Growth creates complexity.

It happens gradually. A spreadsheet. A shared folder. An email trail. A workaround. Another workaround. Another process.

Every decision makes sense at the time - until information becomes fragmented and nobody can see how work really moves through the business. The business still runs, but it runs on memory, chasing, assumptions and heroic individuals.

The problem is not effort.
The problem is clarity.

The aim is simple: make the work visible.

Questions

Questions people ask.

Is this bespoke software?

Yes. It is not one fixed product. It is built around how your business actually works - not a generic tool, but the screens a team actually needs to run the work.

We have outgrown our spreadsheets. Is Trefn for us?

Probably. Most work starts where good people are fighting bad systems: spreadsheets, shared folders, email trails and three answers to the same question. Trefn moves that into one connected system.

Will it replace the software we already use?

It can. Sometimes the right answer is to connect the software you already use. Sometimes it is to replace the tools that cost more than they give back - one connected system instead of several separate subscriptions can save a business a lot of money. The first conversation works out which.

What kind of businesses is it for?

Growing businesses where work passes between people and teams - for example manufacturing, installation, field service, logistics and wholesale. If the answer to "where is that up to?" lives in spreadsheets, inboxes and people's heads, it is worth a conversation.

Who will we work with?

Ross. You do not get passed from salesperson to account manager to developer. The person understanding the problem is the person creating the system.

Where are you based? Do you come on site?

Trefn is based in Nottingham. Ross works on site or remotely - whichever suits the work - with businesses across the UK.

How does it start?

With the process everyone hates. The first conversation is free, and it is not about software. It is about what is slowing the business down, where information gets lost, and whether Trefn is the right fit.

How is it priced?

Every system is different, so there is no price list. After the first conversation you get a written proposal with a fixed price within a week. No day rates. No estimates that grow.

What happens once it is live?

It keeps improving. The system changes because the business changes, so support is agreed in the proposal - fixes, improvements and changes as the business grows.
