The problem

Growth creates complexity.

It happens gradually. A spreadsheet. A shared folder. An email trail. A workaround. Another workaround. Another process.

Every decision makes sense at the time - until information becomes fragmented and nobody can see how work really moves through the business. The business still runs, but it runs on memory, chasing, assumptions and heroic individuals.

The problem is not effort.
The problem is clarity.

The aim is simple: make the work visible.
