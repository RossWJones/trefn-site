Trial terms

  • The trial is free for 60 days, counted from the day your trial key goes in. No card, and nothing to pay unless you choose to keep it.
  • It runs in your company's Microsoft 365. Your records are kept in a SharePoint site you choose. Trefn's servers don't receive or store them.
  • Your Microsoft 365 admin approves the app and can remove it at any time.
  • When the trial ends, Trefn Complaints turns read only. Everyone can still open and export everything, and nothing is deleted - it's your SharePoint.
  • Keeping it costs £149 a month per company. We confirm the price in writing before you commit.
  • It's a trial: we fix problems as quickly as we can, but there's no guaranteed response time until you keep it.
  • One trial per company. We may say no to a request, for example where we can't support the set-up or there's a conflict with work we already do.
