For IT and security

What Trefn can and can't reach.

For your IT team or provider, before they approve Trefn. Trefn is a web app that signs in as each person and works in your SharePoint with that person's rights. Trefn Ltd never receives your records.

What Trefn can reach.

Trefn asks Microsoft for three delegated permissions. Delegated means it works as the person signed in, so it can only do what they can already do in SharePoint.

Microsoft showsWhat Trefn does with it
Sign in and read user profileSigns each person in and shows their name.
Create, edit, and delete items and lists in all site collectionsSet up makes Trefn's lists on the site you choose. After that, people add and change records in those lists. Trefn also lists the sites a person can open, so it can offer them in Set up and find the site with Trefn on it.
Read all users' basic profilesAsked the first time someone picks a person, for an owner or a sign-off. Names, email addresses and photos only.

Microsoft words the second permission broadly. It can't be limited to one site for an app that works this way, so treat it as covering every site the person can edit. Trefn only reads and writes its own lists. A lock to a single site isn't available yet - if you need one, ask us.

Trefn doesn't ask for access to email, calendars or Teams, and it only works with SharePoint lists.

How the app works.

  • No server code, no databaseThe app is a set of files hosted on Microsoft Azure. Everything happens in the browser, which talks to Microsoft directly.
  • It can only talk to MicrosoftThe app's security policy lets it connect to Microsoft's sign-in and Microsoft Graph only. It can't send your data anywhere else.
  • No analytics or trackingNo tracking cookies or scripts. The app's fonts are its own, so nothing loads from elsewhere.
  • Sign-in for 24 hours at a timeMicrosoft limits the app's sign-in to 24 hours. After that it goes back through Microsoft sign-in, where your policies apply.
  • Encrypted on the wayThe app is served over HTTPS only, and it talks to Microsoft over HTTPS.
  • Updates you can seeWe update the app often. It shows what's new, and version.json shows the version that's live.

Keeping it tight.

  1. Give Trefn its own site.A new team site called "Trefn", with only the people who use it as members. Only people who can open the site can see the records.
  2. Do the site owner step.It makes sign-offs and the investigation diary add only, and Trefn's settings read only for members. About 10 minutes - the setup guide shows how.
  3. Choose who can sign in, if you want to.In entra.microsoft.com → Enterprise applications → Trefn → Properties, set Assignment required? to Yes. Then add people or groups under Users and groups.
  4. Check what happens.Entra's sign-in logs show who signs in to Trefn. SharePoint's version history and recycle bin, and your Microsoft 365 audit log, show changes to the lists.

Removing Trefn.

An admin can remove it at any time: entra.microsoft.com → Enterprise applications → Trefn → Properties → Delete. Nobody can sign in to it after that, and anyone already in is stopped within about an hour, when their access runs out.

The lists stay on your SharePoint site with everything in them. Delete them there if you no longer want them.

If Trefn stops.

Your records don't depend on Trefn's servers. They're ordinary SharePoint lists: you can open, filter and export them in SharePoint without Trefn.

While the app runs, an admin can download everything as one Excel workbook at any time, from Licence → Export everything. That includes every signature and diary entry.
